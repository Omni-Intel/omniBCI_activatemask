For V18 long-run BLE continuity, flash the matching firmware:

SRB1:
firmware/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB1_BLE/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB1_BLE.ino

SRB2:
firmware/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB2_BLE/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB2_BLE.ino

The wire protocol is unchanged, but V18 separates capture/packing from BLE DATA notify so a BLE-stack stall cannot directly starve frameQueue.
