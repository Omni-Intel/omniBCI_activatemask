V16 GUI can still decode the compact reliable BLE V2 stream used by V13/V14.

However, for another Windows PC (Huawei/Lenovo/etc.) use the V16 BLE firmware,
because the important cross-PC fix is also on the ESP32-C3 side:
- no fixed 180 ms blind retransmission;
- ACK-driven adaptive retry timeout;
- in-flight adaptive TX pacing.

firmware/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB1_BLE/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB1_BLE.ino

SRB2:
firmware/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB2_BLE/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SRB2_BLE.ino

firmware/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SERIAL_STABLE/ESP32C3_ADS1299_SERIAL_STABLE.ino
